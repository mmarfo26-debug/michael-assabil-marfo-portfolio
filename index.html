Undergraduate project · 2024

Design and Experimental Evaluation of an Intelligent Multi-Sensor Cyber-Physical Fire Monitoring and Early Warning System

My undergraduate project explored a practical fire-monitoring prototype that combines sensor inputs, embedded processing, and alert mechanisms to help communicate potential fire conditions.

  • Integrated an Arduino Nano with an MQ-2 smoke sensor and flame sensor.
  • Used an LCD and buzzer for local status indication and alerts.
  • Integrated GSM messaging and audio notifications for remote and audible warning.
  • Worked across sensor acquisition, threshold-based decision logic, hardware integration, and prototype testing.
Embedded systemsMulti-sensor integrationEarly warningCyber-physical systems

Engineering connection: The project gave me practical exposure to the sensing-to-decision-to-alert chain found in cyber-physical systems. It also strengthened my interest in reliable monitoring, event detection, and system-level validation.

Next steps I would like to explore: more systematic sensor calibration, documented false-alarm and detection tests, reliability evaluation, and data-driven event classification.
